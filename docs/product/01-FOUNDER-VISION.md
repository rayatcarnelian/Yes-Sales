# 01 — Founder vision and boundaries

**Status:** Normalized product vision. Living document.  
**Date:** 3 November 2026  
**Source:** Founder brief V3.0, reconciled with the current repository.

## What the company is

YES SALES is intended to become a subscription software business. A business owner should be able to sign up, choose a small set of AI employees, supply approved business knowledge, connect channels they control, test the workflow, turn it on, watch the work, and pay every month.

The product sells business outcomes: answered enquiries, captured booking or quotation requests, fewer missed conversations, and a record the owner can trust. It does not sell model names, agent counts, or a demonstration of autonomy.

The long-term shape is one platform with many isolated businesses. It is not a new bot deployment for each customer, and it is not seven named Grok agents.

## What is true today

The repository is a single-organization brokerage operating system. It is a useful internal tool and a source of engineering patterns. It is not yet the customer product described above. No subscriber billing, tenant isolation, website chat, or proven live channel exists in this tree.

Manual client acquisition for the founder's web-development services is a separate activity. It can fund and inform the product. It is not evidence that a business has agreed to pilot YES SALES OS.

## First customer, first job

The first market to test is automotive detailing, coating, polishing, and closely related Malaysian service shops. Names discussed in outreach are conversations, not customers.

The first job to sell is one workflow:

**Answer approved questions, capture the enquiry, and prepare a booking or quotation request that a person confirms.**

The assistant must not invent prices, confirm a calendar slot, or send a message the business has not authorized.

## Boundaries

| Horizon | Allowed | Not allowed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Now | Audit, strategy, reliability fixes inside the current app, and a design for one pilot workflow | New industries, voice, payments, a visual AI office, a general agent marketplace |
| Next | One pilot business, website chat, approved knowledge, enquiry capture, owner review | Claiming real-time booking, live WhatsApp, or a second industry before the first template repeats |
| Later | More roles, official WhatsApp, calendar availability, billing, a second vertical | Unlimited AI usage, unattended research, per-customer Grok subscriptions |
| Rejected until evidence changes | Forking the app per industry, scraping WhatsApp Web, autonomous financial commitments, rebuilding HighLevel | Treating competitor feature lists as a backlog |

## Non-negotiable product rules

1. A button, field, or adapter is not a working integration.
2. Prompts do not grant permissions. The server does.
3. One business never sees another business's data.
4. AI text does not become approved business knowledge by itself.
5. Mock, skipped, and untested are not the same as passed.
6. Predicted revenue is not collected revenue.
7. Grok bots outside this repository are not connected to this application.
8. Legal, privacy, and security work is part of the product, not a later polish pass.

## Success for the first release

One real shop completes real enquiries through one configured workflow, the owner can correct the knowledge and take over a conversation, the operating cost is measured, and the owner is willing to keep paying. A demo is not that result.
